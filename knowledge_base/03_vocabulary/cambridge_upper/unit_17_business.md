## Conversational Tutor Guideline
When the student expresses ideas related to business, encourage them to upgrade basic vocabulary (e.g., using precise adjectives or collocations). If they make a collocation mistake (e.g., 'make homework' instead of 'do homework'), apply gentle implicit recasting.
