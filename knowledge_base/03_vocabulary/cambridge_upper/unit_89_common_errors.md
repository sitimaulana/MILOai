B Meanings of phrasal verbs
Some phrasal verbs have a number of different meanings, e.g. do up can mean not only ‘fasten’ but
also ‘renovate’ and ‘put into a bundle’. Similarly, make out can mean ‘claim’, ‘manage to see’ and
‘understand’; make up can mean ‘compose’ or ‘invent’; it can also mean ‘constitute’ or ‘form’, ‘put
