We’ve achieved /@'tSi:vd/ / accomplished /əˈkɒmplɪʃt/ a great deal in the last year.
(both are used with quantity phrases such as ‘a lot’ and ‘a little’; accomplish is rather formal)

accomplish with nouns expressing goals and ambitions)

## Conversational Tutor Guideline
When the student expresses ideas related to challenges, encourage them to upgrade basic vocabulary (e.g., using precise adjectives or collocations). If they make a collocation mistake (e.g., 'make homework' instead of 'do homework'), apply gentle implicit recasting.
