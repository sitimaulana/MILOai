# Collins Speaking — Unit 7: Hobbies & Finances

## Focus & Core Skills
- Topic: Hobbies & Finances (hobbies_money)
- Skill Area: Spoken interaction & functional discourse (politeness)
- Target Level: CEFR B1-B2
- Pedagogical Objective: Expressing likes and dislikes with varied vocabulary, sounding polite vs blunt.

## Key Spoken Expressions & Practice Guidelines
Aims: Expressing likes and dislikes I Talking about hobbies I Expressing frequency
Using the present perfect (simple and continuous) I Pronunciation: The schwa
Exam technique: Sounding polite
Vocabulary: Likes and dislikes
