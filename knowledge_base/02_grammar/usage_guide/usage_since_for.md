# English Usage Dilemma: Since vs. For (Duration with Perfect Tenses)

## Grammatical Rule & Distinction:
'Since' is used with a specific point in time in the past when the action started (e.g., since 2020, since yesterday, since Monday). 'For' is used with a duration of time (e.g., for three years, for two hours).

## Spoken Examples:
- 'I have been studying English since 2021.'
- 'She has worked here for five months.'

## Common Learner Mistakes to Correct:
Saying 'I am living here since three years' instead of 'I have lived / have been living here for three years'.

## Tutor Recasting Directive:
If the student makes this mistake during conversation, gently recast the correct form in your reply and ask an engaging follow-up question.
