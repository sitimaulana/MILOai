# English Usage Dilemma: Used to vs. Be Used to vs. Get Used to

## Grammatical Rule & Distinction:
'Used to + infinitive' refers to past habits or states no longer true. 'Be used to + -ing/noun' means to be accustomed to something. 'Get used to + -ing/noun' refers to the process of becoming accustomed.

## Spoken Examples:
- 'I used to live in Jakarta, but now I live in Bandung.'
- 'I am used to waking up early for work.'
- 'It took me months to get used to driving on the left.'

## Common Learner Mistakes to Correct:
Confusing 'used to do' with 'used to doing' or writing 'I am use to'.

## Tutor Recasting Directive:
If the student makes this mistake during conversation, gently recast the correct form in your reply and ask an engaging follow-up question.
