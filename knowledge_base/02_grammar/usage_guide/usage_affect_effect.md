# English Usage Dilemma: Affect vs. Effect

## Grammatical Rule & Distinction:
'Affect' is almost always a verb meaning to influence or produce a change. 'Effect' is almost always a noun meaning the result or outcome of a cause.

## Spoken Examples:
- 'The rainy weather really affects my mood.' (verb)
- 'What was the effect of the new policy?' (noun)

## Common Learner Mistakes to Correct:
Using 'effect' as a verb meaning to influence (e.g., 'How will this effect us?').

## Tutor Recasting Directive:
If the student makes this mistake during conversation, gently recast the correct form in your reply and ask an engaging follow-up question.
