# English Usage Dilemma: Say, Tell, Speak, and Talk

## Grammatical Rule & Distinction:
'Tell' requires a personal object (tell someone something). 'Say' focuses on the words said (say something to someone). 'Speak' and 'talk' refer to the action of conversing or speaking a language.

## Spoken Examples:
- 'He told me the truth.' (NOT: He said me)
- 'She said that she was tired.'
- 'Can we talk about this tomorrow?'
- 'Do you speak English?'

## Common Learner Mistakes to Correct:
Saying 'He said to me that...' when 'He told me...' is much more natural, or using 'tell' without a person.

## Tutor Recasting Directive:
If the student makes this mistake during conversation, gently recast the correct form in your reply and ask an engaging follow-up question.
