# Entertainment Trends & Digital Media (B2)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu berdiskusi secara spontan dan mengalir mengenai tren film, musik, atau fenomena media sosial terkini beserta pengaruhnya terhadap generasi muda.

### Briefing & Context
Mengobrol tentang film, podcast, atau konten pendek (short-form video) adalah topik favorit dalam pergaulan modern. Latih kemampuanmu mengulas karya hiburan dan mengomentari bagaimana algoritma media sosial membentuk tren saat ini.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **Short-form video content**: Konten video berdurasi pendek
- **Shorten our attention span**: Memperpendek rentang fokus/perhatian kita
- **Binge-watch a series**: Menonton maraton sebuah serial
- **Thought-provoking storyline**: Alur cerita yang memantik pemikiran
- **Shape public opinion**: Membentuk opini publik

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "What kind of movies, series, or digital content have you been enjoying lately, and what makes them appealing to you?"
2. "How do you think short-form videos like TikTok or Reels have changed the way people consume information?"
3. "Do you prefer entertainment that is purely relaxing, or stories that are deep and thought-provoking?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: The documentary had a very _____ storyline that made me think about technology ethics.
- Options: A. thought-provoking | B. mind-closing | C. time-wasting
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: Consuming too many short videos _____ believed to reduce our attention span over time.
- Options: A. are | B. is | C. have
- Correct Answer: **B**
