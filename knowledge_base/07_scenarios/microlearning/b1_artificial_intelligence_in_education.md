# Artificial Intelligence in Education (B1)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menyampaikan opini sederhana beserta alasan logis mengenai manfaat dan batasan penggunaan kecerdasan buatan (AI) dalam perkuliahan.

### Briefing & Context
AI kini menjadi bagian dari keseharian mahasiswa, namun penggunaannya perlu disikapi dengan bijak. Latih cara menyampaikan pendapatmu mengenai kapan AI membantu efisiensi belajar dan kapan AI justru menghambat kemandirian berpikir.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **In my opinion, AI tools can be beneficial for...**: Menurut pendapat saya, perangkat AI dapat bermanfaat untuk...
- **On the other hand, relying too much on...**: Di sisi lain, terlalu bergantung pada...
- **Enhance learning efficiency**: Meningkatkan efisiensi belajar
- **Brainstorming ideas**: Mengumpulkan/memantik ide
- **Critical thinking skills**: Kemampuan berpikir kritis

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "What is your opinion on students using AI tools like ChatGPT for their university assignments?"
2. "How do you personally use AI to help you study without becoming overly dependent on it?"
3. "What is one negative impact if students rely 100% on AI to do their coursework?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: AI can help students _____ new ideas for their essays quickly.
- Options: A. brainstorm | B. overthink | C. memorize
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: If students _____ too much on AI, their critical thinking skills will decline.
- Options: A. relied | B. rely | C. will rely
- Correct Answer: **B**
