# Presenting Data & Project Outcomes (B2)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menginterpretasikan tren angka, grafik, atau hasil pengujian sistem, serta menjelaskan signifikansi temuan tersebut menggunakan kosakata akademik.

### Briefing & Context
Saat mempresentasikan hasil riset, jangan sekadar membacakan angka di layar, melainkan jelaskan artinya. Gunakan kata kerja analitis untuk mendeskripsikan kenaikan, penurunan, atau korelasi dari hasil pengujianmu.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **As illustrated by the test results...**: Sebagaimana ditunjukkan oleh hasil pengujian...
- **There was a significant increase/decrease in...**: Terdapat peningkatan/penurunan yang signifikan pada...
- **The findings indicate a strong correlation between...**: Temuan ini menunjukkan korelasi yang kuat antara...
- **Margin of error / Accuracy rate**: Batas kesalahan / Tingkat akurasi
- **Demonstrate the effectiveness of...**: Mendemonstrasikan efektivitas dari...

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Imagine your system testing showed an accuracy increase from 70% to 92%. How would you present this finding in a seminar?"
2. "What factors usually explain a sudden drop or spike in experimental data?"
3. "How would you summarize the overall significance of your research outcomes in two sentences?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: The experimental results _____ that the new algorithm runs 30% faster.
- Options: A. indicate | B. speculate | C. guess
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: There has been a _____ improvement in user response time since the last update.
- Options: A. significance | B. significantly | C. significant
- Correct Answer: **C**
