# Handling Behavioral Questions (STAR Method) (B2)

## Track: Job Interview Practice

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menceritakan pengalaman menyelesaikan masalah atau konflik tim secara runtut menggunakan kerangka Situation, Task, Action, dan Result (STAR).

### Briefing & Context
Pertanyaan behavioral menguji cara nyatamu menghadapi krisis di masa lalu. Gunakan metode STAR: awali dengan konteks masalah (Situation & Task), jelaskan langkah spesifikmu (Action), dan tutup dengan dampak positifnya (Result).

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **We encountered a major bottleneck when...**: Kami menghadapi kendala utama ketika...
- **To address this issue, I took the initiative to...**: Untuk mengatasi masalah ini, saya mengambil inisiatif untuk...
- **My primary objective was to...**: Tujuan utama saya adalah untuk...
- **As a direct result of our actions...**: Sebagai hasil langsung dari tindakan kami...
- **Mitigate the risk**: Meminimalkan risiko

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Tell me about a time when your team faced a tight deadline or a technical obstacle. What was the situation?"
2. "What specific actions did you take to help your team overcome that challenge?"
3. "What was the final outcome, and what did you learn from that incident?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: To prevent project delays, I _____ the initiative to reorganize our daily tasks.
- Options: A. made | B. took | C. did
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: By the time the deadline arrived, we _____ all the system bugs.
- Options: A. had resolved | B. have resolved | C. resolve
- Correct Answer: **A**
