# Weaknesses & Personal Growth (B2)

## Track: Job Interview Practice

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mengidentifikasi satu kelemahan profesional secara jujur namun strategis, serta memaparkan langkah konkret yang sedang dilakukan untuk memperbaikinya.

### Briefing & Context
Saat ditanya tentang kelemahan, pewawancara ingin melihat tingkat kesadaran diri (self-awareness) dan kemauan belajarmu. Hindari jawaban klise; sebutkan kendala nyata yang sedang aktif kamu perbaiki beserta progresnya.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **An area I am actively working to improve is...**: Area yang sedang aktif saya perbaiki adalah...
- **I sometimes tend to be overly critical of...**: Saya terkadang cenderung terlalu kritis terhadap...
- **To overcome this, I have started implementing...**: Untuk mengatasi hal ini, saya telah mulai menerapkan...
- **Room for improvement**: Ruang untuk perbaikan/berkembang
- **Constructive feedback**: Umpan balik yang membangun

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "What is one professional skill or habit that you feel you still need to improve?"
2. "How has this weakness affected your academic or project work in the past?"
3. "What concrete steps are you currently taking to turn this weakness into a strength?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: I always welcome _____ feedback from my mentors to improve my performance.
- Options: A. destructive | B. constructive | C. instructive
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: I used to struggle with public speaking, but now I am getting used to _____ in front of large groups.
- Options: A. present | B. presented | C. presenting
- Correct Answer: **C**
