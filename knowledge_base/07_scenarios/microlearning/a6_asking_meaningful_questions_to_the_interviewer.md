# Asking Meaningful Questions to the Interviewer (B2)

## Track: Job Interview Practice

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mengajukan pertanyaan balik yang kritis dan relevan mengenai ekspektasi peran, alur kerja tim, serta budaya perusahaan di akhir sesi wawancara.

### Briefing & Context
Mengajukan pertanyaan di akhir wawancara membuktikan antusiasme dan pemikiran kritismu terhadap posisi yang dilamar. Tanyakan hal substantif seperti indikator kesuksesan di posisi tersebut atau dinamika kolaborasi tim.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **Could you elaborate on the day-to-day responsibilities of...**: Bisakah Anda menjelaskan lebih rinci mengenai tanggung jawab harian dari...
- **What does success look like in the first three months?**: Seperti apa tolok ukur keberhasilan pada tiga bulan pertama?
- **How would you describe the engineering/work culture here?**: Bagaimana Anda mendeskripsikan budaya kerja di sini?
- **Professional development opportunities**: Peluang pengembangan profesional
- **Cross-functional collaboration**: Kolaborasi lintas divisi/tim

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "We are at the end of the interview. Do you have any questions regarding the role or our team?"
2. "What aspect of a company's work culture matters most to you when applying for an internship or job?"
3. "If you wanted to ask the interviewer about how they measure performance, how would you phrase that question?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Could you _____ more on how the product team collaborates with engineers?
- Options: A. elaborate | B. explain | C. describe
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: I would like to know what _____ in the first month of this internship.
- Options: A. are the main priorities | B. the main priorities are | C. do the main priorities
- Correct Answer: **B**
