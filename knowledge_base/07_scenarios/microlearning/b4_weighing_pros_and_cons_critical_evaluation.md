# Weighing Pros and Cons (Critical Evaluation) (B2)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mengevaluasi dua sisi dari suatu isu kompleks (seperti privasi data atau regulasi digital) secara objektif sebelum menarik kesimpulan akhir.

### Briefing & Context
Berpikir kritis di level B2 menuntutmu untuk tidak memihak secara buta. Bandingkan keuntungan dan kerugian suatu kebijakan secara berimbang menggunakan kata penghubung kontras sebelum memberikan kesimpulan akhir.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **On the one hand... on the other hand...**: Di satu sisi... di sisi lain...
- **The primary advantage of this approach is...**: Keunggulan utama dari pendekatan ini adalah...
- **Nevertheless, a major drawback to consider is...**: Meskipun demikian, kelemahan utama yang perlu dipertimbangkan adalah...
- **Weighing both sides of the argument...**: Menimbang kedua sisi argumen...
- **Data privacy concerns**: Kekhawatiran terkait privasi data

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Companies collect user data to personalize apps, but this raises privacy concerns. What are the pros and cons of this practice?"
2. "Do you think the convenience of personalized digital services outweighs the risk to personal privacy?"
3. "How should governments or tech developers balance innovation with user data protection?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: One major _____ of free digital platforms is the risk of personal data leaks.
- Options: A. drawback | B. benefit | C. breakthrough
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: _____ strict regulations are necessary, they should not stop technological innovation.
- Options: A. Despite | B. While | C. Because of
- Correct Answer: **B**
