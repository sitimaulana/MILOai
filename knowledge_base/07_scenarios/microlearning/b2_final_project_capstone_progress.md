# Final Project (Capstone) Progress (B1)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu memaparkan progres tugas akhir/skripsi secara ringkas, meliputi apa yang sudah diselesaikan, apa yang sedang dikerjakan, dan target selanjutnya.

### Briefing & Context
Saat bimbingan atau seminar progres, dosen pembimbing membutuhkan laporan yang jelas dan terstruktur. Gunakan urutan kronologis: apa yang sudah tuntas minggu lalu, kendala saat ini, dan rencana minggu depan.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **So far, I have completed the...**: Sejauh ini, saya telah menyelesaikan...
- **Currently, I am in the middle of...**: Saat ini, saya sedang berada di tahap...
- **The next milestone is to...**: Target capaian berikutnya adalah...
- **Data collection / System design**: Pengumpulan data / Perancangan sistem
- **On track with the schedule**: Sesuai dengan jadwal yang direncanakan

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Could you give me a brief update on what your final project or research is about?"
2. "What part of the project have you completed so far, and what are you working on this week?"
3. "Are there any technical difficulties you need to solve before your next milestone?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Everything is going well, and my final project is currently on _____.
- Options: A. way | B. road | C. track
- Correct Answer: **C**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: I _____ the literature review chapter, and now I am designing the system.
- Options: A. have already finished | B. already finish | C. am already finishing
- Correct Answer: **A**
