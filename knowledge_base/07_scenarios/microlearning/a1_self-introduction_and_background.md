# Self-Introduction & Background (B1)

## Track: Job Interview Practice

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu memperkenalkan latar belakang pendidikan, fokus bidang studi, dan ketertarikan karir dalam 3–4 kalimat terhubung secara percaya diri.

### Briefing & Context
Perkenalan diri adalah penentu kesan pertama di hadapan rekruter. Fokuskan jawabanmu pada nama, jurusan kuliah saat ini, keahlian utama yang sedang ditekuni, dan tujuan karirmu ke depan.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **I am currently a final-year student majoring in...**: Saat ini saya mahasiswa tingkat akhir jurusan...
- **My academic focus has been on...**: Fokus akademik saya adalah pada...
- **I have developed a strong passion for...**: Saya telah membangun ketertarikan yang kuat pada...
- **Hands-on experience**: Pengalaman praktik langsung
- **Career aspiration**: Cita-cita/target karir

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "To start off, could you tell me a little bit about yourself and your educational background?"
2. "Why did you choose to study your current major?"
3. "How does your study background align with the role you want to pursue after graduation?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: I am currently majoring _____ Informatics Engineering.
- Options: A. in | B. at | C. on
- Correct Answer: **A**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: Over the past two years, I _____ a strong foundation in programming.
- Options: A. build | B. built | C. have built
- Correct Answer: **C**
