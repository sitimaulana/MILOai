# Agreeing & Disagreeing Politely (B1)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menyetujui sebagian pendapat rekan diskusi sekaligus menyampaikan sanggahan atau sudut pandang berbeda secara santun dan akademis.

### Briefing & Context
Dalam diskusi ilmiah, menyanggah pendapat orang lain secara langsung ('You are wrong') terdengar kasar. Gunakan teknik hedging—akui dulu poin lawan bicaramu, lalu sampaikan sudut pandangmu dengan sopan.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **I see your point, however...**: Saya paham maksud Anda, namun...
- **That is a valid argument, but have we considered...**: Itu argumen yang valid, tetapi sudahkah kita mempertimbangkan...
- **I agree up to a point, yet...**: Saya setuju sampai batas tertentu, akan tetapi...
- **From a different perspective...**: Dari sudut pandang yang berbeda...
- **I couldn't agree more.**: Saya sangat setuju.

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Some people say that online classes are much better than offline classes in every way. How would you politely disagree with this?"
2. "If a teammate suggests a project idea that is too expensive to build, how would you express your concern politely?"
3. "Why do you think a mix of both online and offline learning is actually more effective?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: I agree with your idea _____ to a point, but we still need to check our budget.
- Options: A. in | B. up | C. on
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: I understand your perspective; _____, the data shows a different trend.
- Options: A. although | B. despite | C. however
- Correct Answer: **C**
