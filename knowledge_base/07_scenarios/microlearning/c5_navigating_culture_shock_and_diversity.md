# Navigating Culture Shock & Diversity (B2)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu bercerita dan berdiskusi secara reflektif mengenai pengalaman beradaptasi di lingkungan baru, perbedaan budaya, serta pentingnya sikap terbuka (open-mindedness).

### Briefing & Context
Bertemu orang-orang dari daerah atau negara berbeda sering kali menghadirkan kejutan budaya (culture shock). Mari berlatih menceritakan proses adaptasi sosial dan bagaimana keberagaman memperkaya cara pandang kita.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **Step out of my comfort zone**: Keluar dari zona nyaman saya
- **Adjust to a new cultural norm**: Menyesuaikan diri dengan norma budaya yang baru
- **Keep an open mind**: Menjaga pikiran tetap terbuka
- **Language and communication barriers**: Hambatan bahasa dan komunikasi
- **Embrace diversity**: Merangkul keberagaman

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Have you ever experienced a small culture shock when moving to a new city or meeting people from different backgrounds?"
2. "What is the most challenging part of adapting to an unfamiliar social or academic environment?"
3. "How does working with people from diverse cultures help us grow personally and professionally?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Studying abroad forces you to step out of your _____ zone and become more independent.
- Options: A. safe | B. relax | C. comfort
- Correct Answer: **C**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: At first, it was strange for me, but I gradually got used to _____ in a multicultural team.
- Options: A. work | B. working | C. worked
- Correct Answer: **B**
