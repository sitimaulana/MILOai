# Travel Experiences & Favorite Places (B1)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mendeskripsikan tempat wisata yang pernah dikunjungi serta menyampaikan rencana destinasi liburan impian di masa depan.

### Briefing & Context
Berbagi cerita perjalanan selalu membuat percakapan menjadi hidup dan seru. Ceritakan suasana tempat favoritmu, kuliner atau pemandangannya, serta satu tempat yang sangat ingin kamu kunjungi suatu hari nanti.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **One of the most memorable places I've visited is...**: Salah satu tempat paling berkesan yang pernah saya kunjungi adalah...
- **It is well-known for its breathtaking views.**: Tempat itu terkenal dengan pemandangannya yang menakjubkan.
- **Sample the local cuisine**: Mencicipi kuliner lokal
- **On my bucket list**: Ada di dalam daftar impian saya
- **A getaway from the city bustle**: Pelarian sejenak dari hiruk-pikuk kota

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "What is the best trip you have ever taken, and what made that place so memorable?"
2. "When you travel to a new city, what do you enjoy doing the most—sightseeing, trying local food, or relaxing?"
3. "Which destination is at the top of your travel bucket list right now?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Visiting Japan during the cherry blossom season has always been on my _____ list.
- Options: A. basket | B. pocket | C. bucket
- Correct Answer: **C**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: It was the most peaceful beach I _____ in my life.
- Options: A. have ever visited | B. ever visit | C. am ever visiting
- Correct Answer: **A**
