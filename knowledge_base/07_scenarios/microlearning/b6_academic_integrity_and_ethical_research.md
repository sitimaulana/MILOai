# Academic Integrity & Ethical Research (B2)

## Track: Academic Discussion

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mendiskusikan pentingnya integritas akademik, etika pengambilan data riset, serta cara menghindari plagiarisme dalam penulisan ilmiah.

### Briefing & Context
Kualitas sebuah riset tidak hanya diukur dari kecanggihan hasilnya, tetapi juga kejujuran prosesnya. Mari berlatih mendiskusikan prinsip sitasi yang benar, persetujuan partisipan (informed consent), dan bahaya manipulasi data.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **Uphold academic integrity**: Menjunjung tinggi integritas akademik
- **Proper citation and paraphrasing**: Sitasi dan parafrasa yang tepat
- **Intellectual property**: Hak kekayaan intelektual
- **Obtain informed consent from participants**: Mendapatkan persetujuan sadar dari partisipan riset
- **Data fabrication and plagiarism**: Pemalsuan data dan plagiarisme

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Why is academic integrity so crucial when writing a thesis or publishing a research paper?"
2. "What are the ethical steps a researcher must take before collecting personal data from human participants?"
3. "How can university students effectively avoid unintentional plagiarism when reviewing literature?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Researchers must always obtain informed _____ from users before recording their personal data.
- Options: A. concept | B. consent | C. content
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: Failing to cite your sources properly _____ considered a breach of academic ethics.
- Options: A. is | B. are | C. were
- Correct Answer: **A**
