# Work-Life Balance & Managing Stress (B2)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu mendiskusikan isu burnout, kesehatan mental, serta strategi menjaga keseimbangan antara tuntutan akademik/pekerjaan dan kehidupan pribadi.

### Briefing & Context
Tekanan tugas akhir dan persiapan karir sering kali memicu kelelahan mental (burnout). Di topik ini, kamu akan berlatih mengungkapkan cara mengenali tanda-tanda stres dan menetapkan batasan (boundaries) yang sehat.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **Experience academic/work burnout**: Mengalami kelelahan mental akibat kuliah/pekerjaan
- **Set healthy boundaries between...**: Menetapkan batasan yang sehat antara...
- **Feel overwhelmed by expectations**: Merasa kewalahan oleh ekspektasi/tuntutan
- **Prioritize mental well-being**: Memprioritaskan kesehatan/kesejahteraan mental
- **Maintain a sustainable pace**: Menjaga ritme kerja yang berkelanjutan

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Why do you think so many university students and young professionals struggle with burnout today?"
2. "What are the early signs that you are feeling overwhelmed, and how do you usually cope with it?"
3. "How can someone set healthy boundaries when working from home or studying digitally?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: When you have too many tasks at once, it is normal to feel _____.
- Options: A. overjoyed | B. overwhelmed | C. overlooked
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: It is essential that every student _____ their mental well-being alongside their grades.
- Options: A. prioritize | B. prioritized | C. prioritizing
- Correct Answer: **A**
