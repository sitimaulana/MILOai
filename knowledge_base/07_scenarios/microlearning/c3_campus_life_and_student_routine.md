# Campus Life & Student Routine (B1)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menceritakan rutinitas harian di kampus, dinamika kegiatan organisasi/komunitas, serta cara mengatur waktu belajar.

### Briefing & Context
Kehidupan kampus penuh dengan cerita menarik, mulai dari kelas pagi hingga mengerjakan tugas bersama teman. Latih kelancaranmu mendeskripsikan jadwal harianmu dan bagian apa yang paling kamu sukai dari menjadi mahasiswa.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **A typical day for me starts with...**: Hari-hari biasanya bagi saya dimulai dengan...
- **Juggling classes and organization activities**: Membagi waktu antara kelas dan kegiatan organisasi
- **Hang out between lectures**: Bersantai bersama di sela-sela jam kuliah
- **Pull an all-nighter**: Begadang semalaman untuk menyelesaikan tugas
- **Manage my schedule**: Mengatur jadwal saya

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "What does a typical busy day at university look like for you?"
2. "Where do you and your friends usually hang out when you have a long break between lectures?"
3. "How do you manage your time when multiple assignment deadlines happen in the same week?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: I try to manage my time well so I don't have to pull an _____ before exams.
- Options: A. all-dayer | B. all-nighter | C. over-timer
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: Sometimes it is challenging to _____ both academic tasks and student club activities.
- Options: A. juggle | B. juggling | C. juggled
- Correct Answer: **A**
