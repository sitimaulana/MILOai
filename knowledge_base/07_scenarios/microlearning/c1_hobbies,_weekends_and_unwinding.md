# Hobbies, Weekends & Unwinding (B1)

## Track: Daily Conversation

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menceritakan aktivitas di waktu luang dan cara melepas penat (unwinding) setelah minggu perkuliahan yang padat dengan ekspresi kasual yang alami.

### Briefing & Context
Membicarakan hobi adalah cara paling mudah untuk mencairkan suasana (ice-breaking) dalam pergaulan. Gunakan ekspresi natural untuk menceritakan kegiatan favoritmu di akhir pekan dan mengapa hal itu membuatmu rileks.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **In my free time, I really enjoy...**: Di waktu luang, saya sangat menikmati...
- **My go-to way to unwind is...**: Cara andalan saya untuk melepas penat adalah...
- **Recharge my batteries**: Mengisi ulang energi/beristirahat
- **Catch up with friends**: Berkumpul/mengobrol santai dengan teman
- **Take my mind off uni work**: Mengalihkan pikiran sejenak dari tugas kuliah

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "After a long and busy week at university, what is your go-to way to unwind on the weekend?"
2. "Do you prefer spending your free time indoors alone, or going out with friends? Why?"
3. "Is there any new hobby or skill you have been wanting to try lately?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: Listening to music helps me take my _____ off stressful assignments.
- Options: A. head | B. brain | C. mind
- Correct Answer: **C**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: On weekends, I usually prefer staying at home to _____ out to crowded malls.
- Options: A. go | B. going | C. went
- Correct Answer: **B**
