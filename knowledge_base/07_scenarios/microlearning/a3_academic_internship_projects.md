# Academic/Internship Projects (B1)

## Track: Job Interview Practice

### Can-Do Statement
Mahasiswa mampu menjelaskan proyek kuliah atau pengalaman magang yang pernah dikerjakan, termasuk peran spesifik dan teknologi/metode yang digunakan.

### Briefing & Context
Pengalaman proyek menunjukkan kesiapan kerjamu meskipun kamu belum lulus. Jelaskan tujuan proyekmu secara singkat, peran apa yang kamu pegang, dan hasil akhir yang berhasil dicapai.

### Target Vocabulary & Key Phrases
- **I was responsible for...**: Saya bertanggung jawab atas...
- **During my internship at...**: Selama masa magang saya di...
- **We developed a prototype of...**: Kami mengembangkan purwarupa dari...
- **My main contribution was...**: Kontribusi utama saya adalah...
- **Successfully implemented**: Berhasil diimplementasikan

### MILO Conversation Prompts (One-Question Flow)
1. "Could you tell me about an interesting academic project or internship you have worked on?"
2. "What was your specific role and responsibility in that project?"
3. "What was the most valuable technical or practical skill you learned from that experience?"

### Micro-Quiz
**Question 1 (Vocabulary)**: In that project, I was responsible _____ designing the user interface.
- Options: A. to | B. for | C. with
- Correct Answer: **B**

**Question 2 (Grammar)**: Last semester, our team _____ a web-based attendance system.
- Options: A. develops | B. developing | C. developed
- Correct Answer: **C**
