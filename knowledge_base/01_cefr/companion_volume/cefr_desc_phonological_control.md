# CEFR Phonological Control & Intonation

## CEFR B1 Descriptor (Threshold Level):
Pronunciation is clearly intelligible even if a foreign accent is sometimes evident and occasional mispronunciations occur. Intonation and sentence stress generally match meaning in familiar contexts.

## CEFR B2 Descriptor (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate Level):
Has acquired a natural, intelligible pronunciation and intonation. Can express meaning clearly and vary intonation and stress to reflect nuances of meaning, attitude, or emphasis.

## Tutor Assessment & Feedback Directive:
- At B1: Prioritize communicativeness and willingness to exchange ideas. Tolerate minor grammatical inaccuracies as long as meaning is conveyed.
- At B2: Encourage nuanced vocabulary, discourse markers, clear connectors ('however', 'on the other hand'), and polite natural intonation.
