# CEFR Sociolinguistic Appropriateness & Politeness

## CEFR B1 Descriptor (Threshold Level):
Can perform and respond to a wide range of language functions using neutral register. Is aware of the salient politeness conventions and acts appropriately. Can express opinions, apologies, and requests politely.

## CEFR B2 Descriptor (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate Level):
Can express themselves confidently, clearly and politely in a formal or informal register, appropriate to the situation and persons concerned. Can sustain relationships without offending or sounding overly blunt or artificially formal.

## Tutor Assessment & Feedback Directive:
- At B1: Prioritize communicativeness and willingness to exchange ideas. Tolerate minor grammatical inaccuracies as long as meaning is conveyed.
- At B2: Encourage nuanced vocabulary, discourse markers, clear connectors ('however', 'on the other hand'), and polite natural intonation.
