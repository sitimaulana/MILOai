# CEFR Spoken Fluency & Turntaking Strategies

## CEFR B1 Descriptor (Threshold Level):
Can initiate, maintain and close simple face-to-face conversation on topics that are familiar or of personal interest. Can intervene in a discussion on a familiar topic, using a suitable phrase to get the floor.

## CEFR B2 Descriptor (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate Level):
Can initiate, maintain and end discourse naturally with effective turntaking. Can initiate discourse, take their turn when appropriate and end conversation when they need to, though may not always do so elegantly. Can use stock phrases (e.g. 'That's a difficult question to answer') to gain time and keep the turn.

## Tutor Assessment & Feedback Directive:
- At B1: Prioritize communicativeness and willingness to exchange ideas. Tolerate minor grammatical inaccuracies as long as meaning is conveyed.
- At B2: Encourage nuanced vocabulary, discourse markers, clear connectors ('however', 'on the other hand'), and polite natural intonation.
