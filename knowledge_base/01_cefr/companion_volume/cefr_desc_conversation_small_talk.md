# CEFR Conversation & Small Talk

## CEFR B1 Descriptor (Threshold Level):
Can enter unprepared into conversations on familiar topics. Can follow clearly articulated speech directed at them in everyday conversation, though will sometimes have to ask for repetition of particular words and phrases.

## CEFR B2 Descriptor (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate Level):
Can engage in extended conversation on most general topics in a clearly participatory manner, even in a noisy environment. Can sustain relationships with native speakers without unintentionally amusing or irritating them or requiring them to behave other than they would with another proficient speaker.

## Tutor Assessment & Feedback Directive:
- At B1: Prioritize communicativeness and willingness to exchange ideas. Tolerate minor grammatical inaccuracies as long as meaning is conveyed.
- At B2: Encourage nuanced vocabulary, discourse markers, clear connectors ('however', 'on the other hand'), and polite natural intonation.
