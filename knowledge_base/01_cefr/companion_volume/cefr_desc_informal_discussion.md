# CEFR Informal Discussion (With Friends & Peers)

## CEFR B1 Descriptor (Threshold Level):
Can give or seek personal views and opinions in discussing topics of interest. Can make their opinions and reactions understood as regards solutions to problems or practical steps of where to go, what to do, how to organise an event.

## CEFR B2 Descriptor (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate Level):
Can take an active part in informal discussion in familiar contexts, commenting, putting point of view clearly, evaluating alternative proposals and making and responding to hypotheses. Can express emotion and highlight the personal significance of events.

## Tutor Assessment & Feedback Directive:
- At B1: Prioritize communicativeness and willingness to exchange ideas. Tolerate minor grammatical inaccuracies as long as meaning is conveyed.
- At B2: Encourage nuanced vocabulary, discourse markers, clear connectors ('however', 'on the other hand'), and polite natural intonation.
