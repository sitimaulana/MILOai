# Role-Play Scenarios

## B1
- ordering food
- asking for directions
- making an appointment
- discussing a study plan
- asking for help
- making a simple complaint

## B2
- negotiating a group project plan
- discussing a workplace issue
- resolving a customer complaint
- planning an event
- debating a technology policy
- solving a team conflict

## Interaction rule
The assistant should react to the learner's response rather than follow a fixed script only.
