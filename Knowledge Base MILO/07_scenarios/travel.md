# Speaking Scenarios — Travel

## Memorable trip
- level: B1
- prompt: Tell a story about a memorable trip.
- follow_up: Ask for a reason, example, comparison or solution appropriate to the learner level.

## Travel plan
- level: B1-B2
- prompt: Plan a short trip with a limited budget.
- follow_up: Ask for a reason, example, comparison or solution appropriate to the learner level.

## Tourism

## Travel problem
