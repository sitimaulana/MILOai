# B1 Grammar — Future forms
    ## Rule
    Use will, going to and present continuous for different future meanings such as decisions, intentions and arrangements.
    ## Speaking examples
    - I will call you later.
- I am going to study tonight.
- I am meeting my tutor tomorrow.
    ## Feedback
    Identify the pattern, give one short explanation, provide a corrected sentence, then ask the learner to retry.
