# CEFR Mediation — MILO Extension

Mediation concerns helping people make meaning or communicate across linguistic, conceptual or
cultural barriers.

## Potential MILO tasks
- explain a short piece of information in simpler English
- summarize another person's idea
- relay instructions
- compare two pieces of information
- explain a concept to another learner

## Scope
Mediation is an optional future module for MILO because the primary research focus is speaking
practice and engagement.
