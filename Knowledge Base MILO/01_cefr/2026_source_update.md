# 2026 Source Update and CEFR Scope

## Current framework status
As of 2026, the Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume remains the current extended CEFR
reference used for this knowledge-base design. The Companion Volume was published in 2020 and
extends the 2001 CEFR descriptor set.

The Council of Europe describes additions including:
- mediation
- online interaction
- plurilingual/pluricultural competence
- expanded phonological competence
- updated listening and reading descriptors
- sign-language competence

## MILO relevance
MILO primarily targets spoken interaction and speaking practice, but the additional dimensions
are useful for future expansion and for digitally mediated interaction.

## Source priority
1. Council of Europe for CEFR constructs and proficiency descriptors.
2. English Profile for research-based English grammar/vocabulary level information.
3. British Council for learner-facing B1–B2 practice content.
4. Cambridge English for example task formats and speaking-performance assessment resources.

This hierarchy prevents pedagogical examples from being mistaken for official CEFR descriptors.
