# CEFR Online Interaction — MILO Extension

The CEFR Companion Volume includes online interaction descriptors that address communication in
digitally mediated environments.

## MILO relevance
MILO is a digitally mediated speaking environment. Useful interaction behaviours include:
- responding to a remote conversational partner
- managing turn-taking
- clarifying meaning
- reacting to messages or prompts
- maintaining interaction when communication becomes difficult

## Design implication
Online interaction can be treated as a supporting competency rather than a replacement for
face-to-face spoken interaction.
