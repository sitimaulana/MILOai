# B1–B2 Interaction Strategies

## Taking the floor
B1: I think..., In my opinion...
B2: I'd like to add..., Another point worth considering is...

## Clarification
Could you repeat that? / What do you mean by...? / Could you explain that?

## Confirmation
So, you mean that...? / If I understand correctly...

## Repair
Sorry, I mean... / Let me rephrase that. / What I was trying to say is...

## Agreement
I agree. / I agree to some extent...

## Disagreement
I don't really agree because... / I see your point, but...

## Follow-up
MILO should use follow-up questions to promote elaboration instead of ending practice immediately.
