# MILO Knowledge Base — Expanded CEFR B1–B2

This expanded MVP is designed for an AI English speaking-practice system.
It separates proficiency descriptors from instructional content and feedback rules.

Collections:
01 CEFR: proficiency targets and speaking descriptors
02 Grammar: B1/B2 grammar knowledge, errors, examples and speaking prompts
03 Vocabulary: thematic vocabulary, collocations, functional language
04 Speaking: interaction, fluency, coherence and task design
05 Pronunciation: intelligibility-oriented support and ASR precautions
06 Feedback: response policies and correction rules
07 Scenarios: conversation tasks across everyday and academic contexts
08 RAG: metadata, chunk schema, retrieval and prompt grounding
09 Assessment: engagement and speaking-support measurement

The material is a research-prototype knowledge base. It is not a CEFR certification instrument.
CEFR proficiency claims should be verified against the Council of Europe source; level-specific
English grammar/vocabulary claims should be verified against English Profile or other authoritative
level-specific evidence before being used as formal assessment criteria.
