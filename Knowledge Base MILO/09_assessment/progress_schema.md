# MILO Progress Data

## Session-level
- session_id
- learner_id (pseudonymous in research datasets)
- timestamp
- level_target
- topic
- task_type
- duration
- completion

## Interaction-level
- turn_number
- transcript
- ASR confidence if available
- retrieved_knowledge_ids
- feedback_type
- retry_requested
- retry_completed

## Engagement indicators
- session frequency
- session duration
- completed tasks
- retry behaviour
- streak
- progress
- badges

Do not interpret logs alone as proof of learning or CEFR attainment.
